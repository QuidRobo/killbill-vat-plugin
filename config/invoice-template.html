{{! Kill Bill VAT invoice template. One file, five cases, driven by the flags killbill-vat-invoice-formatter exposes: invoice.domesticVat VAT at the supplier's own rate. An ordinary home sale. invoice.destinationVat VAT at the CUSTOMER's country rate: EU B2C under the One Stop Shop, or a country with a local registration. The money is that country's VAT, not the supplier's, and the invoice says so. invoice.reverseCharge 0%, EU business customer accounts for the VAT itself. invoice.outsideScope 0%, place of supply outside the supplier's country. invoice.zeroRated 0% to a domestic customer, e.g. a fully credited invoice. invoice.vatTreatmentUnknown No tenant id configured, so the treatment cannot be established and no legal statement is made. Fix the deployment. invoice.standardVat is still available and is true for domesticVat or destinationVat. Without that formatter installed the totals block and VAT summary collapse to nothing, because Mustache skips sections whose key does not resolve. The invoice still renders; it just is not a compliant VAT invoice. Upload it in Kaui: your name, top right, Tenant Configuration, Invoice Template, Choose File. Either set the LOGO_INFO tenant key, which fills {{logo.logo}}, or replace that reference with a data: URI so the invoice needs no external fetch. A data URI is usually the better choice: it renders identically in a browser, in a PDF and in an email client that blocks remote images. 4. The wording of the notices below, and the OSS registration number in the letterhead, which only appears on a destination-VAT invoice.
